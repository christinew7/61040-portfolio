# Application Pitch

CrochetBuddy removes the friction from following crochet patterns, making it effortless with following crochet patterns, whether you're used to US or UK terms. CrochetBuddy features an abbreviation reference, US/UK terminology toggle, and a row tracker! <br>

1. Abbreviation Reference: There will be an inline explanation on what the abbreviation is that the crocheter can reference to in the pattern. The crocheter can access this by highlighting or clicking the term. This way, crocheters don't need to google or scroll out of the instruction to understand what a term means and pattern designers don't need to clog up their patterns with explanations.
2. US/UK Terminology Toggle: Since the primary terminology in crochet patterns uses either the US/UK terminology, a simple toggle on the screen lets crocheters instantly switch between US and UK terms, making it easier to understand a pattern in what the crocheter is familiar with. This will reduce confusion and mistakes since crocheters don't have to mentally translate as they crochet.
3. Row Tracker: Crocheters can mark off each completed row and highlight their current row in the pattern, dimming the rest of the pattern. This eliminates the mental effort to remember what row the crocheter is on and the frustrating task to recount to find your place after an interruption.
