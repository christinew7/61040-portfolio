# Application Pitch

## Name

### Motivation
<!-- summarize the probelm that application will solve in a sentence -->
no friction on crocheters; make it easier to follow crochet patterns whether you're used to US/UK terms (also just add easier to follow anything everything)

### Key features
<!-- explain up to three features in a brief narrative, ensuring that each has a name and a simple explanation of what the feature is; why it helps mitigate the problem; and how it impacts stakeholders.  -->

1. Abbreviation Reference: There will be an inline explanation on what the abbreviation/term is that the crocheter can reference to in the pattern. The crocheter can access this by highlighting or clicking the term. This way, crocheters don't need to google or scroll out of the instruction to understand what a term means and pattern designers don't need to clog up their patterns with explanations.
2. US/UK Terminology Toggle: Since the primary terminology in crochet patterns uses either the US/UK terminology, a simple toggle on the screen lets crocheters instantly switch between US and UK terms, making it easier to understand a pattern in what the crocheter is familiar with. This will reduce confusion and mistakes since crocheters don't have to mentally translate as they crochet.
