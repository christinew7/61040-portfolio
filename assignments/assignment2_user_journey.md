# User Journey

Bobbert is a beginner crocheter. He's been learning how to crochet using US terminology by watching American YouTubers. In his next project, Bobbert wants to crochet a beanie from a UK crocheter and the pattern only exists as a written pattern -- there is no video tutorial. He begins to follow the pattern but he keeps getting confused with the UK terminology because a US single crochet is a UK double crochet and a US double crochet is a UK treble crochet. It's entirely different from what he learned and is used to. He keeps losing his place, constantly switching between browser tabs to Google translations and recounting his rounds in frustration. He decides to search for an app to help with his problem, and he succeeds! He finds [CrochetBuddy](/assignments/assignment2_ui.md#home-page).

Bobbert opens the [CrochetBuddy](/assignments/assignment2_ui.md#home-page) app and logs in to land in the [first-time user welcome page](/assignments/assignment2_ui.md#home-page-first-time-user). He notices that he needs to manually add the pattern on his own so he clicks "Upload Pattern" on the same welcome page. Under the [Upload Pattern](/assignments/assignment2_ui.md#upload-pattern) page, he gives the project a title under the "Title" field and copies and pastes the written pattern under the "Pattern" field. He finishes the process by hitting "Submit" at the bottom of the page. He gets to the [pattern's page](/assignments/assignment2_ui.md#default-pattern-page) and clicks the language button so he can switch from UK to US terminology. The page refreshed to replace the UK terms with the US terms, as seen in the [annotated page](/assignments/assignment2_ui#pattern-switch-language). Bobbert remembers that he just finished a 'tc' (treble crochet) in round 2, but now it's written as a 'dc' (double crochet).

Bobbert is relieved that he doesn't need to Google the translations anymore. He also realizes he can do a simple tap on unfamiliar abbreviations to reveal the full phrase without leaving the pattern, as seen in the [abbreviation wireframe](/assignments/assignment2_ui.md#pattern-abbreviation). This helps him crochet even faster and avoids cluttering the page with the full phrases for every abbreviation. He finally notices the light button to the right of the language button, which automatically dims the screen to highlight the first round of instructions. He clicks the down arrow on the right side of the page to get to his current round, the second round, as seen in the [row tracking wireframe](/assignments/assignment2_ui.md#pattern-row-tracker).

Bobbert finishes his beanie and feels like he saved so much time by using the CrochetBuddy app because it automatically translates everything for him, even shows what the abbreviated terms mean, and helps him keep track of where in the pattern he's at. He can actually focus on the joy of crocheting rather than the frustration of translation.
